# BeyondSea

Project page: **Flexible Perception as Extra Eyes for Ships in Port**.

Live page: https://vbeyvl.github.io/beyondsea/

Ships in busy ports rely on what their crews and onboard sensors can see. Blind spots do occur: a large vessel or a quay
structure can hide a small boat. BeyondSea studies flexible perception, giving a ship information about the areas it
cannot easily see for itself. The films on the page show the starting point: cameras on shore detect a craft hidden from
a ship and share its position.

All footage is simulation in a virtual model of the Port of Liverpool, played at twice real speed.

- `index.html` – the page
- `media/` – one clip per scenario and a short teaser (1280x720)
